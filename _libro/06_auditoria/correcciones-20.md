## Limpiezas globales

### Frases de proceso eliminadas o reescritas

**Capítulo 39: 7 frases.**

1. "the habits themselves are developed elsewhere in the book and are not reconstructed here".
2. "(The unit records that he illustrates the point with a treadmill example; the details are not recoverable from the material.)".
3. "The material available for this chapter does not include Koe's detailed development of the third pillar…".
4. "(the details of which belong to the video's opening and are not reproduced here)".
5. "much of this material is philosophy of life" → "much of what Koe says on these subjects…".
6. "the 2026 unit itself" → "the 2026 passage itself".
7. "turns all input into material" → "raw material for the project". Es un cambio preventivo para evitar la ambigüedad.

**Capítulo 40: 5 frases.**

1. "(of which the corpus excerpt preserves the trap of treating youth as currency…)".
2. "the practical material on health" → "the practical advice on health".
3. "the material on relationships" → "what the corpus says about relationships".
4. "the spiritual material" → "the spiritual passages".
5. "is the unit of the collective" → "is the basic building block of the collective". La palabra era legítima, pero se cambió para que la verificación quede limpia.

En los dos capítulos se conservan los usos legítimos de "material" en el sentido de lo material o lo físico: "material things", "spiritual materialist", "material world", "immaterial", etc.
