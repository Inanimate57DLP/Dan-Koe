## Limpiezas globales

### Vocabulario del proceso editorial

En total se reescribieron 42 frases o expresiones: 32 en el capítulo 09 y 10 en el capítulo 10.

**Capítulo 09 (32):**

- **Menciones al léxico (12).** Ejemplos:
  - "a phrase that the lexicon records as his own coinage" → "a phrase of his own coinage";
  - "the lexicon lists it as his" → "his own";
  - "The lexicon records this as his *philosophy of building*" → "He calls this his *philosophy of building*".
- **Menciones al registro de evolución (11).** Ejemplos:
  - "The evolution record of the corpus classifies this as a refinement" → "Read across the corpus, this is a refinement";
  - "The evolution record judges these complementary" → "These versions are complementary".
  - Incluye la frase de EV-195, reescrita como "Read across the whole corpus, from the 'long-term game' of 2023 to the guest's factory job of 2025…".
- **"Corpus record" como sustantivo (4).** Por ejemplo, "according to the corpus record, possibly reached him" → "possibly reached him".
- **"One unit" en sentido de proceso (2).** Por ejemplo, "the corpus treats the two as one unit" → "the two are versions of one procedure".
- **"Cluster of capacities" (1)** → "set of capacities".
- **"Practical unit" (2).** El título de §9.3 pasa a "The Project as the Practical Vehicle" y "the practical unit of change" a "the practical vehicle of change". Así se sigue el criterio del lote 04, que ya cambió "practical unit of change" en los capítulos 07 y 08.

**Capítulo 10 (10):**

- **Celda de tabla (1).** "Mentioned in the corpus record; not developed in this chapter" (hallazgo 5).
- **"The record of these versions is a refinement" (1)** → "Taken together, the versions are a cumulative refinement".
- **"The corpus record treats these as two answers" (1).** Se reescribió dentro del hallazgo 6.
- **"The corpus record classifies it as a terminological… conflict" (1)** → "the conflict is best read as terminological".
- **"The corpus record flags the conflict" (1)** → "the conflict should be stated plainly".
- **"Which the corpus records" (1).** Se eliminó.
- **"In his lexicon" / "In Koe's lexicon" (2)** → "In his vocabulary" / "In Koe's vocabulary".
- **"Four of them are developed in the material of this chapter… emotional transmutation is not developed in these sources" (1).** Se reescribió así: "Four of them are developed in this chapter: …; the corpus does not develop emotional transmutation further".
- **"Practical unit" (1)** → "practical vehicle", en la introducción.

Se conservaron los usos ordinarios de "material" ("material goals", "material needs", "material items", "make the immaterial material") y de "assigned to" ("assigned to you by your employer"). También se conservó "the corpus records" como verbo en lenguaje de libro, por ejemplo "The corpus records a later shift".
