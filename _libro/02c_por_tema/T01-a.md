### subtema: actualization hierarchy

## U-023-053
- **tipo:** concepto
- **titulo:** Two types of hierarchy: dominator hierarchies (pyramid schemes) vs natural/actualization hierarchies (increasing wholeness)
- **desarrollo:** Everything is structured as a hierarchy, of two types. (1) Dominator hierarchies, which give the word hierarchy a bad reputation: pyramid-scheme-esque structures. (2) Natural hierarchies or actualization hierarchies: in order of increasing wholeness, where the whole on one level becomes part of the whole of the next. Society mimics hierarchical structure, but society (context dependent) falls under dominator hierarchy. Its parts — public schools, government, corporate jobs, even some religious institutions (more culture, but still part and whole) — are structured this way: "hierarchies within hierarchies". Main feature: more people with less power at the bottom, fewer people with more power at the top; in a true pyramid scheme the investors at the bottom enable this power distribution.
- **ejemplos:** Natural: particles → atoms → cells → organisms; letters → words → sentences → paragraphs. Dominator: public schools, government, corporate jobs, religious institutions.
- **cita:** "there are more people with less power at the bottom and then there are less people with more power at the top"
- **terminos:** dominator hierarchies; natural hierarchies; actualization hierarchies; increasing wholeness; hierarchies within hierarchies
