## U-013-019
- **tipo:** método
- **titulo:** How to generate a unique perspective: brain dump problems, benefits, goals, personal experiences, then explain to a friend
- **desarrollo:** Based on your topic, brain dump: common problems associated with the topic; benefits from overcoming those problems; common goals associated with it ("what are you helping people achieve" — where how-to advice comes in: what goal you help them achieve and what problems stand in the way); your personal experiences related to it. Then write it as if you were explaining how to get to that goal to a friend, with all that context: "I'm trying to get to this goal but the problem standing in the way is this, so how am I going to tell someone how to get there." Mechanism: "a problem creates the perspective." Everyone has different problems; the perspective is "what reality or what angle are you looking at this goal from and achieving this goal from."
