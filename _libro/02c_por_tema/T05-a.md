- **desarrollo:** "I'd recommend the 3 by 20 method. That's what I'm calling it." Walk three times a day, 20 minutes after breakfast, lunch and dinner. You'll drop water weight and inflammation if you've never done it, and notice "a decently drastic change in how you look and feel within the first 7 days," assuming the average American lifestyle and in the context of the whole protocol.
