### subtema: identity lists

## U-002-017
- **tipo:** método
- **titulo:** Identity lists: a pinned phone note where every action toward a desired identity is logged as evidence
- **desarrollo:** Dickie's method for behavior change. Problem: aspirational identity change ("I want to be someone who works four hours a day ... I need to be focused, organized") is hard because "it's not very easy to see tangible progress towards that." Steps: (1) Whenever he wants to start a behavior/change, make a list titled with the identity and pin it as a note on his phone. (2) Define what that identity entails. (3) Every action he takes toward it, write it in the note (e.g., "I plan my day the day before"). (4) Over time this becomes "a big list of evidence" supporting the identity. Mechanism: it "hacked the dopamine" — he takes the action because he knows he gets to write it down; it's "a new source of gamification," reinforcing the belief, and "that flywheel starts to spin more and more." Dan summarizes it: make a goal conscious, change behavior to actualize it, write down each action to reinforce it — Dickie: "you start to do it so you can write it down." Cadence: gather one item per week on each of six or seven identities; a year gives ~50 pieces of evidence. Benefit: "it becomes a source of truth" answering "am I really making progress on this," which otherwise stays vague. After six months he had about a hundred items. Started "this year" (2023).
- **ejemplos:** See U-002-018 (financial abundance identity, valet tip).
- **cita:** "you start to do it so you can write it down ... and it reinforces it"
- **terminos:** identity lists; evidence; flywheel; gamification
- **origen:** de-tercero:Dickie Bush (inspired by James Clear and Alex Hormozi)
- **nivel:** intermedio
- **prerrequisitos:** U-002-016
