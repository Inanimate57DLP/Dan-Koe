## U-025-099
- **tipo:** argumento
- **titulo:** Why the alter ego works: its shift in attention consumes your ~50 bits/second of conscious attention, leaving no bandwidth for self-doubt — "you become what you focus on."
- **desarrollo:** There are many psychological reasons; his main one: when you adopt an alter ego, your mind focuses on how you should act; your finite mental energy (~50 bits of conscious information per second) is consumed by the shift in attention, so you lack bandwidth for self-doubt and insecurities. Conscious mind: 50 bits/s; unconscious: ~11 million bits/s (discussed in his book The Art of Focus). If you give those 50 bits to self-doubt, "it becomes your world you become what you focus on." It takes effort to expand into a new mind. Therefore: create a mental frame to focus attention away from self-doubt and fear (vision); a new identity to expand into with repetition and learning; and a plan so attention narrows further and you live in flow.

- **tipo:** framework
