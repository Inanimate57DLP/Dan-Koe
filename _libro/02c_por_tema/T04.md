### subtema: boredom and anxiety

## U-025-108
- **tipo:** heurística
- **titulo:** Reverse-engineer distraction: when distracted, diagnose whether the task causes anxiety (too hard / lacking knowledge, skill or clarity) or boredom (too easy), and adjust it to the middle.
- **desarrollo:** When attention narrows on a task, distractions are harder to register. But if you don't know what to do next and next, it falls apart: if the next task is hard you get anxious; if too easy you get bored; then attention splits out of the task, the present moment splits from your vision, and distractions register. - **tipo:** ejercicio-del-autor
- **titulo:** The coffee test: drink a ton of coffee and watch your mind zoom in on the smallest things
- **desarrollo:** To become conscious of zooming in real time: drink a ton of coffee and watch your mind zoom in on the smallest little things "because of the stress response". If you induce a stress response (e.g., too much caffeine), you notice little problems, they immerse your attention, and you can't even remember what you were supposed to do later today; "you can't remember the Finer Things in life in that state of stress". It demonstrates that stress narrows attention onto problems.
- **ejemplos:** Too much caffeine.
- **cita:** "you can't remember the Finer Things in life in that state of stress"
- **terminos:** stress response; zoom in
- **origen:** propia
- **nivel:** complementario
- **prerrequisitos:** U-026-049

## U-026-059
- **tipo:** principio
- **titulo:** Your problems are as big as you make them; every problem has an opportunity on the other side of the coin
- **desarrollo:** "Your problems are as big as you make them." You must understand problems are normal, everyone has them, and "there are always two sides to the same coin": if there is a problem there's an opportunity on the other side "that you are not seeing because your mind is not open enough because you're so laser focused on the problem itself".
