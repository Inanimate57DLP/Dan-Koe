## U-005-004
- **tipo:** principio
- **titulo:** Burnout is about loss of control, not about working hard.
- **desarrollo:** Justin explains his burnout: he took his first executive role at 33 as "a stretch hire," expecting to take the company to "a million, maybe two," and instead grew it to about $51 million. "As it got bigger and bigger and bigger, I lost control." His thesis: "burnout is about loss of control. It's not about working hard. I could work hard all day. The implication (which reappears in his life philosophy and in Dan's comments on alcohol) is that regaining control, not reducing effort, is the remedy.
- **ejemplos:** PatientPop scaling from his expected $1-2M to ~$51M while he lost control.
