## U-011-002
- **tipo:** método
- **titulo:** Silent, discerning observation of others as a mirror for self-learning
- **desarrollo:** The author describes himself as having always been a "silent observer" — not judgmental (he admits it was a bit judgmental at first and that it is hard to escape) but "discerning." The practice: silently observe how other people live and discern whether their actions are good or bad, then turn the observation inward. The steps he gives: notice what they do (e.g., what they put in the grocery cart, how they look); ask whether you want to look/be that way; notice "the reflection in yourself" — what reaction the observation causes in you; ask what you can learn from that reaction. The point is not to judge others but to use them as a mirror to learn about yourself and how you want to live.
- **ejemplos:** At the grocery store: observing what people put into their grocery cart, how they look, and whether you want to look that way.
