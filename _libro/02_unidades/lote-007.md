## U-007-016
- **tipo:** framework
- **titulo:** The three things a one-person business needs: brand, content, monetization
- **desarrollo:** (1) Brand: like your audience or your storefront; how you are perceived. A one-person business is branded on yourself: a personal brand, a personal business. The brand revolves around your vision for the future and your goals, which is what you lead people toward, because "followers follow leaders"; if you are not a leader, don't try to start a business. (2) Content: how you attract people to the brand. Content is created from your interests (self-development or skills) by educating people while throwing in your unique spin, personal thoughts, beliefs, maybe mindset, what you do daily. You entertain, educate and inspire, and learn to structure content so it catches attention, delivers value on that attention, and converts attention to a follow, a purchase or an email subscriber, slowly building leverage over time. (3) Monetization: people screw this up. AdSense is fine but not in your control; you can be taken off a platform anytime. You need an email list or your own product or service where you control the price and the traffic; that is what determines your income. You must become valuable enough to sell a product or service to someone one to two steps behind you, or something with a use case in other people's lives.
- **ejemplos:** YouTube AdSense as uncontrolled monetization; coffee brand as controlled product.
- **cita:** "followers follow leaders right so if you are not a leader then don't try to start a business"
- **terminos:** brand; storefront; content; monetize; leverage
