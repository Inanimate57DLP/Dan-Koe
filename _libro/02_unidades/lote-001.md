## U-001-001
- **tipo:** principio
- **titulo:** "Work less, earn more, enjoy life" is the author's personal motto and value proposition, built on the idea that you build systems so you can work less.
- **desarrollo:** The author states that "work less, earn more, enjoy life" is his motto and the value proposition on his website. He frames it as an instance of "success is counterintuitive": forcing himself to try to work less and to build solutions so he can work less, rather than accepting that "you can only work 8 hours a day," has brought him the most growth. The mechanism: working less leads to higher quality rest or more rest, which leads to higher quality work. The three parts: (1) work less — you can work as much or as little as you want if you build the systems that allow it; (2) earn more — subjective and up to the individual; most people want to earn more doing what they enjoy, and he has a rule that he must build/create a way to do that rather than expect it to fall into his lap; over time anyone with dedication, will and consistency can do what they enjoy for life and make a good income; (3) enjoy life — usually a byproduct of working less, resting more, earning more, and having time and location freedom to do what you choose.
- **ejemplos:** Contrast between "4-Hour workday" people and hustle-and-grind entrepreneurs working 12–16 hours a day.

## U-001-021
- **tipo:** framework
- **titulo:** The four pillars of the one-person business ("experience model"): goals → brand, problems → content, systems → product, benefits → marketing.
- **desarrollo:** Traditional branding, marketing, content and offer advice doesn't fit the one-person business. In this "experience model," "you are your customer Avatar you are your brand you are your Niche you are your content you are your offer you are everything," and self-awareness and self-reliance are the greatest skills. The breakdown: (1) Your goals are your brand — what do you want out of life and what are you leading people towards? (2) The problems standing in the way of your goals turn into content — what roadblocks will followers encounter and how will you help them overcome them (a starting point; more goes into content). (3) Systems, or how you solve the problem, turn into a product — how can they solve their problems faster. (4) The benefit — how this journey impacted your life — turns into marketing — why should they care about your message. Benefits-rich language is extremely powerful and persuasive. Summary: you pursue a goal, solve your own problems, teach others how you did it, and that's your business. It's oversimplified, with many nuances; you must test and figure it out for yourself to have unique, genuine knowledge. Hiring someone to build your brand relinquishes "all potential leverage and just power."
- **ejemplos:** Why he writes every day turned into a persuasive benefits argument: earn an income, think more clearly, systemize all other content — visible on his landing pages.
- **cita:** "you are your customer Avatar you are your brand you are your Niche you are your content you are your offer you are everything"
