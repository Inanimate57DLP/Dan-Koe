## U-015-024
- **tipo:** método
- **titulo:** Step 2: Send an inspired compliment (leveraging the law of reciprocity)
- **desarrollo:** The first message is "an inspired compliment": find a piece of their content or work that truly inspires you, that you like, or that gave you an insight, and send it to them explaining what you liked. "People love praise," and through "the Law of reciprocity," if you give value — and praise is a form of value — they feel obligated to give it in return. Dan's example DM (pretending he wrote a tweet on managing emotions): "What's up Dan, this tweet hit me hard, I've been going through it the past couple of days and this instantly gave me some relief, thank you" plus the link to the post. If they don't respond, reach out again in the same manner with something else you liked, "and keep doing that until they respond."
- **ejemplos:** Script for a tweet on managing emotions with the link attached.
- **cita:** "praise is a form of value"
- **terminos:** inspired compliment, Law of reciprocity
