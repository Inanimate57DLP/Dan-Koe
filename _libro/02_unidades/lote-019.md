## U-019-001
- **tipo:** argumento
- **titulo:** Most people struggle to do good work because they work too much and are stuck in "doing mode"
- **desarrollo:** The author opens by framing overwork as the cause of bad work, not the cure for it. People who work too much have a mind that is "always on", like a car running out of gas pushing down the road to reach some destination. This produces "doing mode", which he describes as a narrow-minded state. When left unchecked, doing mode leads to a "hot ball of stress" in the chest whose source is hard to identify, a feeling of a closed mind, damaged quality time with others, being unappreciative of life's innate richness, and forgetting what it feels like to have a clear line of thought. These people wear overwork as a badge of honor and feel they need to keep working on something without knowing what, because they are stuck in an "imaginary Rat Race" chasing a destination they are not clear on themselves or one that someone else told them to reach.
- **ejemplos:** Metaphor of a car out of gas pushing down the road toward a destination.
- **cita:** "stuck in doing mode, a narrow-minded state"
- **terminos:** doing mode; imaginary Rat Race; badge of honor
- **origen:** adaptada-de:Alex Soojung-Kim Pang (Rest)
- **nivel:** fundamental
- **prerrequisitos:** ninguno
- **fuente:** Minimalist Productivity Work Less, Do More.md [0:00-0:47] (2024-08-11)
- **tension:** Immediately qualified by the author: he is not saying long hours are bad (see U-019-002).
