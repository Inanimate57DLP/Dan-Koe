## U-004-021
- **tipo:** argumento
- **titulo:** What stops creators is a "mindset and awareness gap": moving from "me me me" survival to seeing the platform as an ecosystem of real humans.
- **desarrollo:** John frames it as "the journey of a human being," like "climbing up Maslow's hierarchy of needs": first we're in silos, "me me me," because "I literally need to put food on the table." Then with some success you notice people leaving kind comments (and a couple of mean ones), you feel grateful, want them "to feel seen" — "that's just relationship building in general" — and discover other humans who care about the same things, which brings "giddy, almost childhood joy." Next level of awareness (which can be "utilitarian"): "a more effective business is one where more people are more bought into what you're doing"; you realize "this whole Instagram app is actually an ecosystem" in which creators can work together toward a mission. Also: "every follower isn't worth the same." Conclusion: "you're not just sending your content into the void, there are real human beings on the other end," and the more they feel "seen and heard" and part of something greater, the more effective you are, "purely rationally."
- **ejemplos:** "The ecosystem of Dan" with other successful creators; Emma Chamberlain (see U-004-022).
- **cita:** "you're not just sending your content into the void there are real human beings on the other end"
