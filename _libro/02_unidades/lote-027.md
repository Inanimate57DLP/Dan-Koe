## U-027-001
- **tipo:** argumento
- **titulo:** "Be yourself" is good advice that fails for the masses because most people are someone else, seeing the world through a lens they did not create
- **desarrollo:** Dan Koe opens by listing the contexts in which people hear "just be yourself" (starting to write, starting a personal brand, posting online, approaching a girl for a date). He says it is good advice but not for the masses, because they do not truly understand what it means to be yourself. The reason: they "are somebody else" and view the world through that borrowed lens rather than one they created. The chain of conditioning he lays out: (1) at school they unconsciously adopted bad habits, personality traits and an outlook on the world that defined what is possible for them (usually "not very much"); (2) college is a fresh start but becomes "another round of conditioning" layering over what was already there; (3) at a job they accept the company's goals as their own and build an identity around the job title they were given. The result of this "unconscious misalignment" is dread, despair and "the death of a soul", leaving a "robotic animalistic corpse of a human being" living in a perpetual state of survival, "a slave to the external world."
- **ejemplos:** writing, personal brand, posting online, approaching a girl; school → college → job sequence.
- **cita:** "they are somebody else and they are viewing the world through that lens not the lens that they created"
- **terminos:** be yourself; conditioning; unconscious misalignment; slave to the external world
- **origen:** propia
- **nivel:** fundamental
- **prerrequisitos:** ninguno
- **fuente:** Why You Can't Be Yourself (The Power Of Being Alone).md, 0:00–1:00, 2022-11-13
- **tension:** ninguna
