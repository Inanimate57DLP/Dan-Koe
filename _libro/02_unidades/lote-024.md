## U-024-001
- **tipo:** historia
- **titulo:** The Catalina Island kayaking storm: a boy scout trip that the author uses as the master story for the waves of life
- **desarrollo:** As a boy scout, Dan Koe went to weekly meetings at his church, recited the scout oath, and went on monthly camping trips plus one big yearly trip (Moab, Utah: mountain biking, rappelling, white water rafting). He tells one specific trip to Catalina Island. They drove from Arizona to California in his dad's truck, with a TV placed in the middle seat area and an Xbox hooked to a portable power charger, playing Halo 3 survival mode the whole way. On the ferry, while adults drank bloody marys, the kids played cards and he had a virgin piña colada. They arrived late and rushed straight to the kayak rental, strapping bags to the kayaks to go around the island and camp at different beachfronts. For the first hour or two the waters were beautiful (dolphins or seals swam next to them). Then a storm hit: rain, waves of "at least like 10 feet in difference", repeated flipping at the bottom of the waves, the group got split up, they could only see the scout leaders' orange oars from the top of a wave, and lost sight of those behind them. His bag fell off. They gave up multiple times ("just throw us into the side of the thing"), then got bursts of inspiration near the top of waves when they saw they were closing in on the leaders. It was "a cycle of going fast going slow giving up losing hope thinking we're gonna make it". After two or three hours in the storm, in which he truly thought his life would end, they saw the shore, got "a huge burst of inspiration", paddled as hard as they could, crashed onto the shore, rolled out of the kayaks and lay shivering in the sun. Those who fell behind were brought in by security people navigating the storm; his food got soaked, and the next morning birds had eaten it and scattered it across the campsite. He uses the story "to paint a life concept": the cycles or waves of life.
- **ejemplos:** The whole trip narrative; the highs and lows of paddling mirror "a very good story".
- **cita:** "it was a cycle of going fast going slow giving up losing hope thinking we're gonna make it and all of these highs and lows that emulate a very good story"
- **terminos:** cycles of life; waves of life

## U-024-006
- **tipo:** metáfora
- **titulo:** The mind is a harmonic oscillator: every low is followed by a roughly equal high until equilibrium
- **desarrollo:** Borrowing from classical mechanics, Dan Koe gives "the 80/20" of the harmonic oscillator: it tries to maintain equilibrium; if displaced low it tries to return to equilibrium but overshoots in the opposite direction, so a low brings "an inevitable but somewhat equal high", and it keeps going until it reaches equilibrium again. "Our mind is a harmonic oscillator." There are aspects of life you cannot control. This explains why changes produce swings: a high will be followed by a counter-force low, and vice versa, before settling.
- **ejemplos:** Physics model of the harmonic oscillator.
- **cita:** "our mind is a harmonic oscillator"
- **terminos:** harmonic oscillator; equilibrium

- **titulo:** The mind as a system of feedback loops explains why chasing highs (cheap dopamine) produces lows
- **desarrollo:** Chain: (1) the mind is "a complex set of systems"; (2) systems make decisions through positive and negative feedback, which can be conditioned over time — "that's what makes a good system"; (3) if you have a high high and register it as positive feedback, you will want to reach that high again; (4) but per the harmonic oscillator, or just the inevitable waves, there is a counter-force; (5) so if you pursue those high highs and consistently want "cheap dopamine or those distractions", you will wonder why you keep going into low phases of life — "that is why", especially when you are not mindful of it. If these phases do not affect you, you are "separating an entity, emotion, from your being" and becoming aware of it, navigating it without negative impact. This opens the topic of rewiring, reprogramming or reconditioning the mind for more beneficial outcomes, especially to navigate what you perceive as negative aspects, "which are in fact inevitable aspects of life".

- **cita:** "if you're pursuing those high highs and you consistently want let's say that cheap dopamine or those distractions and you wonder why you're consistently going into a low phase of life that is why"
