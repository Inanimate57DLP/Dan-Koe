## U-013-019
- **tipo:** método
- **titulo:** How to generate a unique perspective: brain dump problems, benefits, goals, personal experiences, then explain to a friend
- **desarrollo:** Based on your topic, brain dump: common problems associated with the topic; benefits from overcoming those problems; common goals associated with it ("what are you helping people achieve" — where how-to advice comes in: what goal you help them achieve and what problems stand in the way); your personal experiences related to it.
