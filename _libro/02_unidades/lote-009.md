## U-009-002
- **tipo:** argumento
- **titulo:** Three reasons agency and freelance work suit beginners: free education, no traffic required, only three to four clients needed
- **desarrollo:** Dan gives the reasons freelancing is marketed to beginners "for good reason." (1) The education is free: with the internet you can learn pretty much any skill you want. The path: improve your skill set over time, iterate on your service, get three to four clients, increase your prices, "and boom you're making more money than ninety percent of the population."

- **titulo:** The three-point micro business model: newsletter, thread, tweet, repeated for years

The structure "newsletter thread tweet" forms your traffic source, your "content ecosystem" that generates traffic to send to your products or services. Most of Twitter's successful people run on this because they're writers; it's a flywheel of long, medium and short form content "holding the most attention and delivering the best ideas to people's minds."

## U-009-138
- **tipo:** principio
- **titulo:** Attract a broad audience and niche down with your product, not your audience; define your terms for beginners (80% of the market)
- **desarrollo:** When writing promotions, understand which part of your audience you're talking to.
