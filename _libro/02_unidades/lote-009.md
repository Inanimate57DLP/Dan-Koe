## U-009-002
- **tipo:** argumento
- **titulo:** Three reasons agency and freelance work suit beginners: free education, no traffic required, only three to four clients needed
- **desarrollo:** Dan gives the reasons freelancing is marketed to beginners "for good reason." (1) The education is free: with the internet you can learn pretty much any skill you want. Building an audience helps "a lot" and he highly recommends doing it from the start, "but you don't need it": you can learn outreach strategies, cold email and LinkedIn prospecting to get clients. The path: improve your skill set over time, iterate on your service, get three to four clients, increase your prices, "and boom you're making more money than ninety percent of the population."

- **cita:** "you only need three to four clients a month right you're not selling a product so you don't need to make like a hundred sales a month"

- **titulo:** The three-point micro business model: newsletter, thread, tweet, repeated for years
- **desarrollo:** The process: (1) start a newsletter; (2) write the newsletter; (3) condense it into a thread, Instagram carousel or any other repurposable format; (4) turn those into tweets and let them go (also repurposed to other platforms); (5) see which perform best; (6) turn the best into a newsletter; (7) repeat the cycle "for years and years and years until you develop a philosophy that people love to read about." Dan's philosophy is "the one person business model and a bunch of philosophical Concepts to back that." The structure "newsletter thread tweet" forms your traffic source, your "content ecosystem" that generates traffic to send to your products or services. Most of Twitter's successful people run on this because they're writers; it's a flywheel of long, medium and short form content "holding the most attention and delivering the best ideas to people's minds."
