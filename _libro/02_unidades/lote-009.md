## U-009-002
- **tipo:** argumento
- **titulo:** Three reasons agency and freelance work suit beginners: free education, no traffic required, only three to four clients needed
- **desarrollo:** Dan gives the reasons freelancing is marketed to beginners "for good reason." (1) The education is free: with the internet you can learn pretty much any skill you want. The path: improve your skill set over time, iterate on your service, get three to four clients, increase your prices, "and boom you're making more money than ninety percent of the population."

- **titulo:** The three-point micro business model: newsletter, thread, tweet, repeated for years
