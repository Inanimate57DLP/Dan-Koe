## CLÚSTER C-T09a-23: Frameworks are training wheels and lenses, not identities
- idea: Ante la objeción "quiero ser auténtico, sin frameworks": escribe lo que quieras, pero organízalo en un framework hasta entender qué hace impactante la escritura, porque hay otra persona del otro lado, porque los frameworks no dañan la creatividad ("creativity thrives with constraints") y porque ya usas uno (tu programación inconsciente). Ser uno mismo "in a structured way". No hay que ser dogmático ("paint between the lines"): frameworks y educación son training wheels y lentes para situaciones concretas, no partes de la identidad (el error del keto como ideología). Para aprender persuasión: investigar frameworks, ponerlos lado a lado, practicar, notar patrones y romper las reglas, quedándose con los principios. Recapitulación: aceptar que necesitas poder y estatus, usar el pyramid principle, estructurar con un framework, hacer outline, capturar ideas, redactar y publicar para obtener datos.
- nivel: fundamental
- evolución: ver E-03.

### U-014-088

- **titulo:** Answer to "what if I want to be authentic, no frameworks?": organize writing into a framework until you understand what makes it impactful
- **desarrollo:** Common objection from his course students, Kortex community, calls and podcasts: "what if I don't want to use a framework, what if I want to be authentic and write whatever I want?" His answer: write what you want, but organize your writing into a framework until you understand what makes writing impactful. Reason 1: another person is on the other side of your writing; if they don't see it as valuable — and the mind makes sense of words in a specific structure — you'll fall short. Reason 2: frameworks don't hurt creativity, they enhance it. Reason 3: you're already using a framework — your mind and how you were programmed to think — but it's unconscious and "clearly unimpactful or else you would have the things you want in life." So "write what you want... like you've been doing and keep getting the results you've been getting."
